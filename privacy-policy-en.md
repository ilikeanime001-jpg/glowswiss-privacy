# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: February 20, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Glow Swiss ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 2. Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.** 

Our app is designed to provide dry cleaning service information and appointment booking functionality. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users.

## 3. Appointment Booking

When you use the appointment booking feature:
- You voluntarily provide your name and phone number
- This information is used solely for the purpose of the current appointment communication
- We do not store or retain this information after the appointment is completed

## 4. Third-Party Services

Our app does not contain any third-party analytics tools or trackers. We do not share any data with external parties.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
